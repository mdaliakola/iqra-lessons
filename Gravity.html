Interactive Physics Derivations

1. Universal Gravitation Formula

$F = G \frac{m_1 \cdot m_2}{r^2}$

Step 1: Force is directly proportional to product of masses ($F \propto m_1 m_2$).
Step 2: Force is inversely proportional to square of distance ($F \propto 1/r^2$).

2. Acceleration Due to Gravity ($g$)

$g = \frac{G \cdot M}{R^2}$

Equating gravitational force with weight ($m \cdot g = G \frac{M \cdot m}{R^2}$). Mass $m$ cancels out!

3. Escape Velocity Formula

$v_{esc} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot G \cdot M}{R}} = \sqrt{2 \cdot g \cdot R}$

Equating Kinetic Energy ($\frac{1}{2} m v^2$) with Gravitational Potential Energy ($\frac{G M m}{R}$).
